SABLE PROTOCOL

A round-based tactical shooter in the foundry of Foundry Nine. You lead the Jackals: plant the Core at the Furnace (A) or the Vault (B), or eliminate the Wardens holding them. Money buys rifles, armour and grenades between rounds — and shots only land true when you stop to aim.

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look · Click Fire
  • Shift Walk (quiet) · Ctrl Crouch
  • R Reload · 12 Weapons
  • E Plant the Core (hold, in a site)
  • F Flash · G Shard grenade
  • B Buy menu · Tab Scores
  • RMB Scope (Verdict sniper)
